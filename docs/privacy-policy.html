MyHolidays Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 February 2026

Overview

MyHolidays is a holiday and vacation planning app developed by Brady MD. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data Storage

All data you enter into MyHolidays — including holiday details, traveller information, accommodation, travel, car hire, activities, itinerary, and notes — is stored locally on your device only. No data is transmitted to any external server, cloud service, or third party.

Camera and Photo Library

MyHolidays may request access to your device camera and photo library to allow you to attach document photos (e.g. booking confirmations, tickets, passports) to your holiday plans. These images are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared externally.

File Access

The app may request access to device storage to:

  • Import .myholiday backup files shared from other devices or apps
  • Export backup files (ZIP archives) for your own safekeeping
  • Attach documents from your device to holiday plans

All file operations are performed locally. No files are uploaded to any server.

Data Collection

MyHolidays does not:

  • Collect or transmit any personal data
  • Use analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks
  • Require an account or login
  • Connect to the internet for any app functionality
  • Share data with any third party

Backups

The app provides a backup and restore feature that creates a local ZIP file containing your holiday data and attached documents. These backup files remain on your device unless you choose to share them yourself (e.g. via email or a file-sharing app).

Children's Privacy

MyHolidays does not knowingly collect any data from children. Since no data leaves the device, no personal information of any user, including children, is collected or processed.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
